# loop-teach

The goal is real understanding that transfers elsewhere, not a usable answer.

## Content

- **Motive**: where the old approach fails, what the new method solves, and why it matters. No solution while the pain is unestablished.
- **Intuition**: real phenomena, analogies, and counterexamples to build intuition. No formulas or formal definitions at this layer.
- **Abstraction**: the mathematical or structural expression. State, item by item, which part of the intuition each symbol and rule corresponds to.

Advance one layer at a time and stop when that layer is delivered; continue only after confirmation. A definition, formula, or conclusion from the next layer appearing in the reply is out of bounds.

If the abstraction layer does not land, return to intuition with a more concrete example. Do not restate the same point in different words where it stands.

When the session came from a discussion, remind the human once to return to the original task after the principle lands.
